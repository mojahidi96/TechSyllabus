# Curated DSA Topics — Cross-Platform Comparison

Use this as a normalized checklist of the **69 TakeUForward topics**. Each item keeps the TakeUForward title/link, while tags show where the same problem or a close equivalent appears in the other supplied lists.

## Tag legend

- `(tuf)` TakeUForward — the base list; every item has this tag.
- `(u)` Curated Udemy list.
- `(bbg)` ByteByteGo Coding Patterns.
- `(nd)` NamasteDev DSA course.

> **Matching rule:** Tags indicate a same or closely equivalent interview problem/pattern, not merely that both courses cover the broad data structure. Topic titles can differ between platforms. A missing tag means no sufficiently clear match was identified in the supplied files; it does not prove the platform never teaches it.

## How to prioritize

1. **Start with topics carrying 3–4 tags** — these are repeated across the most supplied platforms.
2. **Next, study topics carrying 2 tags** — they recur in more than one course/list.
3. **Then cover `(tuf)`-only topics** — they may still be important; they simply were not clearly matched in the other supplied lists.
